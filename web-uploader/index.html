Capabilities

A complete professional toolchain

From ingest to analysis to export — every step engineered for survey workflows.

Precision measurement

Distance, height, area, angle and point coordinates — with custom CRS and global unit switching, results read out in real time.

Volume calculation

Polygon selection with 2.5D grid integration, gap-aware sampling, auto-recommended parameters, and cut/fill colour visualisation.

Clipping & deletion

Box clip, polygon delete regions, and cross-section profiles — multi-layer deletion stays in sync with the backend on export.

Coordinate systems

Built-in WGS84, UTM and Web Mercator. Custom EPSG/PROJ/WKT, plus support for .prj / .gsb / .tif parameter files.

Multi-mode visualisation

RGB, elevation, intensity, classification and LOD colouring; EDL eye-dome lighting and an X-Ray transparency mode.

Profile & section measurement

Run measurements directly inside section views, switch backgrounds for clarity, and annotate with live dimensions.

Honest LAS export

Coordinates rewritten to true CRS metadata. Deleted regions propagate to the backend so what ships is what was reviewed.

SLAM scanner support

Auto-discovers scanner projects, parses geo_info.csv coordinate files, and loads full-site data with trajectories in one click.

Why CloudStudio

Engineered for the field, not the demo.

A cloud-native 3D platform built for survey, construction, and inspection teams who can’t afford to wait on a desktop install.

01 — Self-hostable

Your data, your servers.

Deploy CloudStudio on your own infrastructure in minutes, or run it managed. Encrypted transit, scoped access, multi-project isolation — built for teams that take governance seriously.

02 — Built for scale

Billions of points, no jitter.

A floating-origin pipeline sidesteps the precision walls that plague WebGL viewers. Measurements stay rock-steady at planetary coordinates; overlays and clipping never drift.

03 — Open to anyone

A link is all it takes.

Stakeholders open scenes in any modern browser — no client software, no plugins, no GPU workstation required. Engineers and reviewers see the same thing, instantly.
